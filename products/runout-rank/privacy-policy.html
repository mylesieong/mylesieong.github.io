Privacy Policy — Runout Rank

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Runout Rank is a billiards skill-rating and training app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and what choices you have. It applies to the app as distributed through Google Play (com.municornio.app.tableinfinite) and the Apple App Store (com.municornio.app.tableinfinite.TableInfinite).

1. The short version

  • There is no account. You never give us your name, email address or phone number, because the app never asks for one.
  • Your results stay on your phone. Rating tests, scores, tiers, practice history, training log and settings are stored on the device and are never uploaded to us.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, to understand how the app is used in aggregate.
  • There is no in-app switch for this. Usage statistics are collected for the whole time the app is installed. §5 sets out what you can do about it: device-level controls, deleting the app, and writing to us.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not run advertising. The analytics SDK does collect a resettable advertising identifier as part of its standard behaviour (§4). We do not use it to target ads, build an advertising profile, or share it with advertisers, and you can reset or delete it in your device settings (§5).

2. Who we are

Runout Rank is published by Municornio Ltd., 1075 W 14th Ave, Vancouver, BC, Canada. For anything in this policy, contact us at the address in §9. Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we are the data controller for the limited data described in §4.

3. What stays on your device and never leaves it

The app stores the following locally, in its private app storage:

WhatWhy
Rating Test results, scores and tiers, including tests in progressTo show your rating and let you resume an unfinished test
Practice attempts, per-level run-out rates and streaksTo calculate your trends and progress over time
Training log entries and favouritesSo your own notes and saved drills are there next time
App settings and onboarding stateSo the app opens the way you left it, and the introduction is not shown twice

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no sync and no account recovery, by design. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it, permanently and irrecoverably, including your rating history.

If you have enabled a platform backup — Android Auto Backup or an iCloud device backup — a copy of this local data may be included in your own backup. That backup is controlled by you and your Google or Apple account, not by us, and we have no access to it.

4. What is collected: anonymous usage analytics

To understand how the app is used and where it fails people, Runout Rank uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. Collection runs for as long as the app is installed. The app does not offer a setting to switch it off; §5 explains the options you do have.

What is collected

  • Events describing app usage, such as: the app being opened, screens being viewed, a rating test being started or completed, a practice level being selected, a drill being logged, a result being shared, and the subscription screen being shown or resulting in a purchase, cancellation or error. Session length and frequency are included.
  • Standard technical and approximate-location information collected automatically by Firebase: a randomly generated app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, screen and locale settings, and country or region derived from IP address. Google processes your IP address to derive that region; Firebase Analytics does not store the IP address itself.
  • A resettable advertising identifier. On Android this is the Advertising ID (AAID) provided by Google Play services. On iOS, where available, it is the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV); the app does not request tracking permission, so it does not access the IDFA. Firebase Analytics collects these as part of its standard behaviour, and we have not turned that behaviour off. We use them only to keep measurement consistent — deduplicating installs and grouping the usage events above — never to target advertising, never to build a profile of you, and never in combination with data from any other source.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account identifier — the app holds none.
  • Your precise location. Runout Rank never requests location permission; only a coarse country or region derived from IP address is available to us.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Advertising. The app contains no ads, no ad SDKs, and no ad network integrations. We share nothing with advertisers or data brokers. The advertising identifier described above is collected by the analytics SDK and used only for analytics.
  • The content of your practice in any personally identifying form. That a test was completed at a given tier is collected; who you are is not.

Why

To answer questions we cannot otherwise answer without a backend: whether people who install the app ever finish a first rating test, whether the practice levels are pitched at the right difficulty, where people abandon a test partway through, and whether the app fails more often on some devices than others.

Legal basis (UK/EU GDPR): our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app, under Article 6(1)(f). Where consent is required for analytics identifiers, we rely on the consent you give in installing and using the app. You have the right to object under Article 21, and to withdraw consent; §5 explains how, and what we can do in response.

5. Your choices, and how to exercise them

There is no setting in the app. Runout Rank does not offer an in-app switch for usage data. Collection begins when the app is installed and continues until it is removed.

Deleting the app stops collection. Uninstalling Runout Rank ends all collection from that device immediately and permanently. This is the only way to stop it from the device itself, and it also deletes everything described in §3, including your rating history.

Objecting. If you are in the UK or EU, you may object to this processing at any time by writing to us at §9. We will stop processing the data associated with the installation you tell us about, and delete it, within 30 days — see the paragraph below on what we need in order to identify it.

At the device level: on Android, Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads lets you reset your Advertising ID, which severs the link to everything collected under the old one, or delete it, after which Runout Rank receives a string of zeros instead. On iOS, Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking controls tracking permission — the app does not request it — and Analytics & Improvements ▸ Share iPhone Analytics can be turned off. These settings affect every app on the device, not just Runout Rank. Note that none of them stops the rest of the collection described in §4 — the usage events and the app-instance identifier continue either way. Only removing the app does that.

Deleting what has already been collected: because analytics data is tied to a random app-instance identifier and not to you, we cannot look up "your" data on request. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new identifier and severs any connection to the previous one. If you want the data associated with a specific installation deleted, contact us at §9 — we can delete data by app-instance identifier if you are able to provide it, and can otherwise delete all analytics data for a given date range on request. The request is easiest to fulfil if you have not yet reset your advertising ID.

6. Subscriptions and payment

Runout Rank offers optional paid subscriptions. All payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see, receive or store your card number, billing address, or any payment credential.

We receive from the store only what is needed to unlock and maintain the subscription — the product purchased, its purchase and renewal status, an anonymous purchase or transaction token, and the store country. Depending on the billing provider in use, this may be linked to a pseudonymous app user ID we generate for your installation. The app records that a purchase, cancellation or error occurred, and which plan was involved, as part of the analytics described in §4. It does not record the amount, the payment method, or anything about the account that paid.

Refunds, cancellations and billing questions are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

7. Who your data is shared with

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC), as the provider of Firebase Analytics, and only the data described in §4. Google acts as our data processor for that data. Google may transfer it outside the UK/EEA; those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Google's terms: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple and Google, as the app stores handling subscriptions (§6).

That is the complete list. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics resellers, or any other third party. We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We may disclose data where we are legally required to, or to protect our rights or the safety of users.

8. Retention, security, children, and changes

Retention. Data stored on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Analytics data is retained by Firebase for a maximum of 14 months from collection, after which event-level data is deleted automatically; aggregated, non-identifying statistics may be kept longer. Subscription records are kept for as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet tax, accounting and legal obligations.

Security. Local data is held in the app's private storage, protected by the operating system's app sandbox and, on modern devices, by device encryption. Data sent to Firebase travels over encrypted connections (TLS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children. Runout Rank is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 where local law sets a higher age. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you want any associated data removed, contact us at §9.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may also complain to a supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk). Most of these rights have little to act on here, because the app holds no identifying information about you — but §5 explains what we can do, and we will respond to any request within 30 days.

Changes. If this policy changes materially — in particular if the app ever begins collecting something new, or if our use of the advertising identifier changes — we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes. We will not begin collecting a new category of data without saying so.
